Recommended Diet for Anemia:

🥦 Vegetarian Iron-Rich Foods

(Pair with Vitamin C foods like lemon, orange, or amla to enhance iron absorption)

  • 🌿 Green Leafy Vegetables
    Spinach (Palak), Fenugreek leaves (Methi), Mustard greens (Sarson), Drumstick leaves (Moringa/Sahjan ke patte)
  • 🥬 Pulses & Legumes
    Lentils (Masoor, Toor, Moong dal), Chickpeas (Chana), Kidney beans (Rajma), Soybeans and soy products (Tofu, soy chunks)
  • 🥜 Nuts & Seeds
    Sesame seeds (Til), Pumpkin seeds, Almonds, Walnuts, Cashews, Groundnuts (Peanuts)
  • 🍚 Whole Grains
    Brown rice, Millets (Ragi, Bajra, Jowar), Whole wheat
  • 🥭 Fruits
    Pomegranate, Dates, Raisins, Figs (Anjeer), Apples, Banana
  • 🥗 Vegetables
    Beetroot, Sweet potato, Carrots

🍗 Non-Vegetarian Options (Heme Iron – Easily Absorbed)

  • Eggs (especially yolks)
  • Chicken or mutton liver
  • Lean red meat (mutton, beef)
  • Fish (Sardines, Mackerel – aka Bangda)

🥤 Vitamin C-Rich Foods

  • Amla (Indian gooseberry)
  • Lemon juice
  • Tomatoes
  • Guava
  • Oranges / Mosambi

🥄 Cooking & Eating Tips:

  • Use iron utensils (kadhai/tawa) for cooking – boosts iron content.
  • Avoid tea/coffee right after meals – they reduce iron absorption.
  • Always pair iron-rich meals with a Vitamin C source for better absorption.
